Profile

Gameplay systems built around clear rules.

I work in Luau and focus on the systems underneath gameplay: navigation, decision-making, collision queries, server validation, and repeatable world generation.

My approach is to separate algorithm logic from Roblox adapters, define failure cases early, and verify behavior with deterministic tests. This keeps systems easier to integrate, profile, and review.
